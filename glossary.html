Glossary

Key terms from every week. Quizzes are open-note, so this page is fair game.

51 terms

Access point
Device that lets Wi-Fi devices join a wired network. (Week 3)
Articulation agreement
Agreement between colleges that guarantees how credits transfer. (Week 9)
Authorization
Permission from the owner to access or test a system. The line between ethical hacking and crime. (Week 4)
Availability
Systems and data are there when needed. Part of the CIA triad. (Week 4)
Backup
A copy of data kept separately so it can be restored after loss or ransomware. (Week 4)
CIA triad
Confidentiality, integrity, and availability: the three goals of security. (Week 4)
Cloud
Renting computing resources from a provider instead of owning servers. (Week 3)
Confidentiality
Only authorized people can see data. Part of the CIA triad. (Week 4)
CTF (capture the flag)
A security competition made of puzzles that hide a “flag” to find. (Week 11)
CyberSeek
Free tool showing cybersecurity job postings by location and career pathways. (Week 1)
DevSecOps
Building security into the process of developing and deploying software. (Week 8)
DHCP
Automatically assigns IP addresses to devices that join a network. (Week 3)
DNS
Domain Name System. Translates names like google.com into IP addresses. (Week 3)
Escalation
Handing a problem to a higher support tier with notes on what you tried. (Week 5)
Feeder role
A job, such as help desk or networking, that commonly leads into cybersecurity. (Week 2)
Firewall
Device or software that allows or blocks traffic based on rules. (Week 3)
GDPR
European Union privacy law covering EU residents’ personal data. (Week 8)
GRC
Governance, risk, and compliance: policies, risk management, and audits. (Week 7)
HIPAA
U.S. law protecting health information. (Week 8)
IAM
Identity and access management: who can sign in and what they can reach. (Week 8)
Imposter syndrome
Feeling like a fraud despite evidence of your ability. (Week 12)
Informational interview
A short conversation to learn about someone’s job, not to ask for one. (Week 11)
Integrity
Data is accurate and has not been tampered with. Part of the CIA triad. (Week 4)
IP address
A number that identifies a device on a network. (Week 3)
LAN
Local area network: devices connected in one location. (Week 3)
Least privilege
Giving each person only the access their job needs. (Week 8)
Malware
Any malicious software. (Week 4)
Median
The middle value: half are above and half below. (Week 1)
MFA
Multi-factor authentication: a second proof of identity beyond a password. (Week 4)
NAT
Network address translation: swaps private addresses for a public one at the router. (Week 3)
NICE Framework
NIST’s Workforce Framework for Cybersecurity, describing work roles by tasks, knowledge, and skills. (Week 1)
NJ Transfer
Website showing how New Jersey community college credits transfer. (Week 9)
On-premises
Servers and systems owned and run in an organization’s own building. (Week 3)
PCI DSS
Security standard for organizations that handle payment card data. (Week 8)
Penetration testing
Authorized simulated attacks to find weaknesses. (Week 7)
Phishing
Messages that trick people into clicking, opening, or revealing information. (Week 4)
Post-quantum cryptography
Encryption designed to resist attacks by future quantum computers. (Week 13)
Ransomware
Malware that encrypts files and demands payment. (Week 4)
Responsible disclosure
Reporting a security flaw privately to its owner and allowing time to fix it. (Week 4)
Router
Device that connects networks and chooses paths between them. (Week 3)
Shared responsibility model
In the cloud, the provider secures the platform and the customer secures what they put on it. (Week 8)
SIEM
Security information and event management: collects security events and creates alerts. (Week 7)
SOC
Security operations center: the team that monitors for and responds to attacks. (Week 7)
Social engineering
Manipulating people rather than technology to gain access. (Week 4)
STAR method
Situation, Task, Action, Result: a structure for interview stories. (Week 14)
Switch
Device that connects devices within a LAN. (Week 3)
Ticketing system
Software that tracks support requests from start to finish. (Week 5)
VPN
Virtual private network: an encrypted connection to a trusted network. (Week 4)
WAN
Wide area network: connects LANs across distances. (Week 3)
Work role
In the NICE Framework, a grouping of work defined by tasks, knowledge, and skills. Not the same as a job title. (Week 1)
Zero trust
Security model in which no user or device is trusted by default; every request is verified. (Week 13)
