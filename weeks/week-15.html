  • Commit to specific next steps and continued learning resources. (CLO 3)

Explore

Presentation format

Three to five minutes. Suggested flow: your elevator pitch (30 seconds), the two roles you explored and what you learned (1–2 minutes), your roadmap (1 minute), and one thing you will do next (30 seconds). Slides are optional; your portfolio PDF or this site can be your visual.

Giving useful feedback

Be specific and kind. Name one thing that worked, ask one genuine question, and offer one suggestion the presenter could use tomorrow.

Keep going

Most learning in IT happens after the class ends. TryHackMe, Cisco Networking Academy, NICE’s list of free learning content, professional organizations, and communities like Simply Cyber’s keep you moving. Your next NETW course, starting with NETW 107, is the next step on your roadmap.
