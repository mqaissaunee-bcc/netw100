Explore

Three overlapping fields

Information technology (IT) keeps people and organizations working: laptops, accounts, printers, software, phones, and the help desk that answers when they break. Networking is the plumbing that connects all of it: the switches, routers, Wi-Fi, and cloud links that move data. Cybersecurity protects all of it: the people, the devices, the data, and the network.

In real jobs these blur together. A help desk technician resets passwords (IT), troubleshoots Wi-Fi (networking), and reports a suspicious email (security) before lunch. That overlap is good news: skills you build in one area carry into the others.

Sources: NIST on the 2025 CyberSeek update (opens in a new tab), BLS Occupational Outlook Handbook (opens in a new tab). A median means half of workers earn more and half earn less. It is not a starting salary.

CyberSeek counts real job postings, state by state, and maps career pathways. The BLS Occupational Outlook Handbook reports pay, required education, and 10-year projections for each occupation. You will use both all semester.

A shared language: the NICE Framework

Job titles are messy. One company’s “security analyst” is another’s “SOC engineer.” The NICE Workforce Framework for Cybersecurity, published by NIST, solves this by describing work roles: groupings of tasks, knowledge, and skills. One job can include several work roles, and two people with the same title may do very different work.

As of NICE Framework Components v2.2.0 (released April 2026), the framework organizes 42 work roles into five categories:

Oversight and Governance

Policy, risk, compliance, legal, training, leadership. Where most GRC jobs live.

Design and Development

Architecting and building secure systems and software.

Implementation and Operation

Running systems day to day: technical support, network operations, systems administration.

Protection and Defense

Detecting and responding to attacks: defensive cybersecurity, incident response, vulnerability analysis.

Investigation

Cybercrime investigation and digital evidence analysis.

Heads-up about older sources

Videos and articles from before 2024 describe seven NICE categories with names like “Securely Provision,” “Protect and Defend,” and “Analyze.” Those names were retired. If a source uses them, translate to the five current categories above.
