Explore

Brookdale’s NETW options

Brookdale offers the Network and Information Technology (NTIT) A.A.S. degree and a Cybersecurity certificate. NETW courses include Linux administration, automation, containers, Windows endpoint and hybrid server administration, and perimeter security, and many are built around industry certifications. The college catalog is the official source for the current sequence; your advisor can confirm what applies to you.

Transfer

NJ Transfer shows how Brookdale courses transfer to New Jersey four-year schools. NJ STARS is a state scholarship program for high-achieving New Jersey students that can continue at a four-year school. Articulation agreements are deals between colleges that guarantee how credits transfer. Remember from Week 1: BLS lists a bachelor’s degree as the typical entry education for information security analysts, so many students plan an A.A.S. now and a bachelor’s later, often while working.

The certification map

Four organizations show up in most entry-level postings: CompTIA (A+, Network+, Security+, CySA+), Cisco (CCST, CCNA), ISC2 (CC, then CISSP for experienced professionals), and EC-Council (CEH, an ethical hacking certification). Cloud providers (Microsoft, AWS, Google) and Red Hat have their own tracks.

Stacking credentials

Stacking means each credential builds on the last and each one is useful by itself. A common support-to-security stack: A+ in the first semester, Network+ in the second, Security+ by the end of the first year, while earning college credit and getting a help desk job. Pick a 6–18 month horizon: long enough to make progress, short enough to stay real.

A note on the free ISC2 CC program

ISC2’s One Million Certified in Cybersecurity program ended for new enrollments on May 20, 2026. If you already have a voucher, you have until December 31, 2026 to test. Check the ISC2 CC page for current pricing.
