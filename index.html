    Course learning outcomes

    When you finish NETW 100 you will be able to:

    1. Describe the landscape of IT, networking, and cybersecurity careers using the NIST NICE Framework, including typical roles, responsibilities, and entry requirements.
    2. Explain fundamental concepts in computer networking and cybersecurity at a conceptual, non-technical level suitable for informed career decision-making.
    3. Identify and compare educational pathways, including certificates, associate degrees, and industry certifications (CompTIA, Cisco, ISC2), aligned to personal career goals.
    4. Evaluate regional and national labor market data, including salary ranges, job demand, and growth projections for IT and cybersecurity roles.
    5. Apply ethical reasoning to real-world scenarios involving privacy, authorization, professional responsibility, and the acceptable use of technology.
    6. Produce a Career Exploration Portfolio documenting personal learning, career interests, and a personalized professional development roadmap.
